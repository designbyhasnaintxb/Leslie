02About Leslie

More than just coaching.

Leslie McCoy is a Colorado Springs pickleball coach, organizer and competitor — someone who builds the game around people, not just strokes.

At her busiest she was coaching around 60 players a week, and she kept that pace for roughly two years. She helped open a 24-court indoor club, competes in tournaments, and has built a real network across the local pickleball community.

Now she's shaping what comes next: leagues, events and, possibly, a place of her own.

Coaching was the start. Community is the point.

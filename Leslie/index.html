05Meet Mary Rose

Twenty years of racquets. One game she can't put down.

Mary Rose Parisi is a PPR-certified pickleball coach and a 3.75 player who has been coaching full-time for more than three years across the Capital District.

Before pickleball there were twenty-plus years of tennis, with racquetball and table tennis along the way. That background shows up in how she teaches: clear mechanics, honest feedback and a feel for how points actually unfold.

She coaches at True Pickleball Club, where her clinics are consistently full and most new players arrive the way the best referrals do, by word of mouth.

Credible enough for serious players. Friendly enough for your very first lesson.
